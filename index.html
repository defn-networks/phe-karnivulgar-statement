KARNIVULGAR

// POST-HUMAN SOUND ARTIST //
> ARTIST_STATEMENT

Post-Human Era is not an exhibition but a system in transition. Through noise, rupture, and visual fragments, it maps the erosion of human centrality. This repository contains traces, structures, and unstable documentation — a feedback loop between industrial decay and digital resurrection.

Karnivulgar operates at the intersection of power electronics, death industrial, and post‑humanist theory. Each release is a symptom: environmental collapse, technological psychosis, ritual violence. The mask is not a disguise — it is an interface. A calibration tool for listening beyond the anthropocene.

The work does not ask for empathy. It asks for attention — to the frequencies that remain when the subject withdraws.

> BIO // PROFILE

Karnivulgar is a sound artist and noise practitioner based in Surabaya, Indonesia. Active since [year], their work has been released via Engram Recordings, Silent Method Records, Def Radio, and Tarung Records. They have collaborated with I,Eternal, Abiboss, Angel Kandanane, and others.

Performances and installations often incorporate found footage, damaged circuitry, and real‑time signal processing. The mask — a recurring visual motif — serves as a witness to post‑human ritual, neither human nor machine, but a remnant of both.

In 2026, Karnivulgar presents Post-Human Era at Wisma Jerman, Surabaya: a three‑day showcase of auditory collapse, live chat archiving, and unstable documentation.

Contact / networks: def-n networks, Lantai Dua Studio, Goethe Institut Surabaya.
